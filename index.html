This product comes with a Service-Only Warranty (1–3 months). No part is covered under warranty.

The arcade box does not get damaged automatically. Damage happens only due to misuse, rough handling, or physical force.

  • Buttons: Not covered. Replacement cost ₹33–₹55 each.
  • Handle / Joystick: Not covered. Must be replaced if broken.
  • Plates / Boards: Not covered. These do not fail on their own.
  • Wires: Not covered. Loose/broken wires can be fixed by any local technician.

3. What IS Covered
Free service/checking/support assistance only. We will guide you or your local technician to fix the issue.

4. Courier Policy
If the product is sent to us for service:

  • Courier charges are paid by the customer.
  • One-side courier: ₹700–₹900
  • Both-side courier: ₹1400–₹1800 (Arcade box weight ~5 kg)

5. Reliability Note
We have sold arcade boxes across India for many years. Till today, not a single major issue has been reported. Buttons usually break only in gaming parlours due to rough use. Your arcade box uses high-quality components and a strong, durable body.

6. Support Guarantee
If any issue ever arises:

  • We will provide complete guidance and support.
  • Local technicians can easily repair buttons/wires.
  • Spare parts are always available with us.

Suggestion:
Local repair is faster and cheaper. Courier-based warranty repair becomes more costly than the part itself. Hence, Service-Only Warranty is the most practical option.
